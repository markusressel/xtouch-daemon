General settings¶

Three sections that you only need when the defaults do not fit: [device] is about the control surface itself, [behavior] about the faders, encoders and meters of all strips, and [aliases] about the names that application strips show. The GUI has them on the page Settings.

[device]¶

Option Default Meaning
name "X-Touch-Ext" Part of the name of the sound card of the control surface, as /proc/asound/cards lists it. The daemon takes the first card whose id or description contains this text, with the same upper and lower case. In the card line 0 [XTouchExt ]: USB-Audio - X-Touch-Ext, the id is XTouchExt and the description is USB-Audio - X-Touch-Ext. The default fits the X-Touch Extender, so you rarely change it.
virtual false Drive the virtual device (the control surface drawn in the GUI) instead of the physical control surface (xtouch-daemon run --virtual-device does the same for one run).
touch_required true Whether a fader changes a value only while the control surface reports a touch. false makes every move count, see below.
touchless_faders [] Faders whose touch sensor does not work, numbered from 1 on the left, see below.
startup "sweep" How the strips light up after a connect, see below.
shutdown "sweep" How the strips go dark when the daemon stops, see below.

A reload takes every option of [device]: with another name or virtual, the daemon connects to the other control surface without a restart, and the strips show the same on it.

Startup and shutdown sequences¶

After a connect the strips light up and their faders come up to their values; when the daemon stops, the strips go dark and their faders go down. startup and shutdown choose how:

Value Startup Shutdown
instant All strips at once. All strips at once.
sweep One after the other from the left, 80 ms apart. One after the other from the right.
curtain From the middle outwards, pair by pair (4 and 5 first, 1 and 8 last). From the outside in.
show One wave of light runs across the strips from the left, in about 1 second. As it passes a strip, the strip lights up fully and its fader rises. Then the light goes off, the fader falls to its value and the strip shows its content. The same wave, from the right. Behind it, the strips are dark and their faders at the bottom. 
[device]
startup = "show"
shutdown = "curtain"

Faders without a touch sensor¶

The daemon changes a value only while you touch the fader (see Hardware), because the motor also moves the faders. With a broken touch sensor the daemon never sees a touch, so the motor moves the fader back to its value shortly after every move you make. touchless_faders lets that fader work without the sensor: a move counts as a touch, and half a second without a move counts as letting go. Count the faders from 1 on the left. The GUI has toggles for it under Settings, Extender.

[device]
touchless_faders = [1]   # the touch sensor of fader 1 is broken

touch_required = false does the same for all faders, with the limits below. Use it also if a working sensor does not notice your finger, e.g. when you push the fader from below.

[device]
touch_required = false   # every move of a fader counts

What it can't do:

  • Resting fingers: a finger that rests on the fader without moving it counts as gone after half a second. If the value changed somewhere else in the meantime (e.g. in the volume control of the desktop), the fader moves out from under your finger.
  • The first 3 seconds after a connect: the X-Touch Extender moves all its faders down by itself, so in these 3 seconds a fader counts a move only with a real touch.

[behavior]¶

Option Default Meaning
max_volume 130 The volume of a fader at the top, 100..200 percent. Above 100 % the audio is amplified in software. See the volume scale. Channels may have their own range.
unity_at 77 Where on the fader 100 % is, 10..95 percent of the travel from the bottom. The scale printed next to the faders of the X-Touch Extender marks 0 dB at about 77. See the volume scale.
encoder_step 1 Percent per encoder step, 1..10. Used by the volume and homeassistant encoders, and as the default step of a value of your own.
reset_volume 100 The volume a push on a volume encoder sets, 0..200. A value above max_volume is an error that xtouch-daemon check-config reports. If a channel has its own range, the push sets the nearest volume inside that range.
meters true Whether the LED meters show audio levels at all. The meter option of a channel turns off a single one.
meter_point "pre_fader" Where the meters take the level: "pre_fader" before the volume and Mute of each strip, so the meter shows what the application plays, even when you turned it down. "post_fader" after them, so the meter shows what you hear. A channel, or the entry of one slot in [slots], may have its own, see Meters.

hold and exclude of the strips of applications are in [pool.apps].

[behavior]
max_volume = 150
encoder_step = 2

The volume scale¶

max_volume and unity_at together say what volume each point of the fader has. The scale printed next to the faders marks 0 dB (the volume the application sends, 100 %) at about 77 % of the travel, the default of unity_at. From the bottom to that mark the fader goes linearly from 0 to 100 %, from the mark to the top from 100 % to max_volume.

  • With the default max_volume = 130 both parts have the same slope (100 / 130 is about 0.77): the fader is linear from 0 to 130 %.
  • With a larger max_volume (up to 200) the part above the mark is steeper.
  • With max_volume = 100, 100 % is at the top and unity_at is ignored.

Examples and a table are at 100 % at the 0 dB mark.

[aliases]¶

The name that a strip shows for an application, in place of the name the sound server reports. It applies to strips that take their application from the pools apps and auto, and to pools of your own with kind = "apps" (Applications). An apps channel shows its label instead. An alias has at most 7 ASCII characters, to fit the LCD; check-config reports a longer one.

[aliases]
"WEBRTC VoiceEngine" = "Discord"
"Chromium" = "Browser"

The key is the application name the sound server reports, with the same upper and lower case. xtouch-daemon list-apps and the GUI's suggestions show it.
