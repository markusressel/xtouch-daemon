Behavior, device and aliases¶

The sections that apply to the daemon and to every strip. The GUI has them on the page Settings.

[device]¶

Option Default Meaning
name "X-Touch-Ext" Part of the name of the extender's sound card, as /proc/asound/cards lists it.
virtual false Drive the virtual device instead of the real one (xtouch-daemon run --virtual-device does the same for one run).
touch_required true Whether a fader changes a value only while the device reports a touch. false makes every move count, see below.
touchless_faders [] Faders whose touch sensor does not work, numbered from 1 on the left, see below.
startup "sweep" How the strips light up after a connect, see below.
shutdown "sweep" How the strips go dark when the daemon stops, see below.

A reload takes them all: with another name or virtual the daemon lets go of the device and connects to the other one, which shows the same.

Startup and shutdown sequences¶

After a connect the strips light up and their faders come up to their values; when the daemon stops, the strips go dark and their faders go down. startup and shutdown choose how:

Value Startup Shutdown
instant All strips at once. All strips at once.
sweep One after the other from the left, 80 ms apart. One after the other from the right.
curtain From the middle outwards, pair by pair (4 and 5 first, 1 and 8 last). From the outside in.
show A wave runs across from the left. Each strip turns white, its ring fills up from the left, its meter goes up and its buttons light up from the top (Rec first); then all of it goes off again the same way, the buttons from the top too. Its fader rises with the light and falls with it, to its value. Behind the wave the strips show what they are, about 1.1 s after the connect. The same wave from the right; behind it the strips are dark and their faders at the bottom. 
[device]
startup = "show"
shutdown = "curtain"

Faders without a touch sensor¶

Only a touched fader changes a value (see Hardware), so a fader whose touch sensor failed goes back to its value shortly after every move. Until it is repaired, touchless_faders lets it work without the sensor: a move counts as a touch, and half a second without a move as letting go. In the GUI these are the toggles of touchless_faders under Settings, Extender.

[device]
touchless_faders = [1]   # the touch sensor of fader 1 is broken

touch_required = false does the same for all faders. The touch sensor does not notice everything that moves a fader on purpose, e.g. a fingertip pushing it from below; with it off, such a push counts too.

[device]
touch_required = false   # every move of a fader counts

What it can't do:

  • Resting fingers: a finger that rests on the fader without moving it counts as gone after half a second. The fader then goes to its value, which is where the finger left it, so you notice nothing, unless the value changes elsewhere meanwhile.
  • The first 3 seconds after a connect: after it was switched on, the device moves its faders down by itself. So in the first 3 seconds after the device was connected, a move counts only with a touch, like on the other faders.

[behavior]¶

Option Default Meaning
max_volume 130 The volume of a fader at the top, 100..200 percent. Above 100 % the audio is amplified in software. 130 puts 100 % at the 0 dB mark of the device with one slope over the whole travel; 100 puts it at the top. Channels may have their own range.
unity_at 77 Where on the fader 100 % is, 10..95 percent of the travel; the device marks 0 dB at about 77. For strips whose range goes above 100 %, see 100 % at the 0 dB mark.
encoder_step 1 Percent per encoder step, 1..10. Used by the volume encoder and by the encoders of homeassistant channels.
reset_volume 100 The volume a push on a volume encoder sets, 0..200, at most max_volume. It stays within the range of the channel.
meters true Whether the LED meters show audio levels at all. The meter option of a channel turns off a single one.
meter_point "pre_fader" Where the meters take the level: "pre_fader" before the volume and Mute of each strip, "post_fader" after them. A channel or a place in a slot may have its own, see Meters.

How long apps and auto keep an application and which ones they never take are hold and exclude of [pool.apps].

[behavior]
max_volume = 150
encoder_step = 2
reset_volume = 100

[aliases]¶

The name to show on an auto slot for an application, at most 7 characters of ASCII:

[aliases]
"WEBRTC VoiceEngine" = "Discord"
"Firefox" = "Firefox"

The key is the application name the sound server reports. xtouch-daemon list-apps shows it, and so do the suggestions of the GUI.
