Strips¶

Each [[strip]] section configures one strip of the device, from the left. Up to eight, or more with collapse. Strips that are not configured are auto strips.

[[strip]]
type = "input"
label = "Mic"
color = "red"

type picks what the strip controls:

type Page
output, input Devices
apps, auto Applications
homeassistant Home Assistant
empty below

Options of every audio strip¶

These options apply to output, input, apps and auto strips. homeassistant strips have only color of them, empty strips none.

Option Default Meaning
color "white" The LCD backlight: off (no backlight), red, green, yellow, blue, magenta, cyan, white. Dark while the strip has nothing to control, unless lit_when_idle is on.
lit_when_idle false Whether the LCD keeps its backlight while the strip has nothing to control: no application of an apps group plays, the device is not there, an auto strip has no application. The fader stays down all the same.
meter true Whether this strip's LED meter shows the level (if behavior.meters is on).
meter_point the layout's, else behavior.meter_point Where the meter takes the level: "pre_fader" before the volume and Mute of the strip, "post_fader" after them, see Meters.
rec "recording" What the LED of the Rec button shows: recording, playing, default, muted or off, see LEDs.
solo "off" What the LED of the Solo button shows, the same values, see LEDs.
min_volume 0 The volume of the fader at the bottom, 0..200 percent.
max_volume behavior.max_volume The volume of the fader at the top, 0..200 percent.
encoder depends on the type What the encoder does, see Encoders.
fader "volume" "homeassistant" hands the fader to Home Assistant, see Encoders.
id "strip_<position>" With fader or encoder in Home Assistant (or choices): part of the entity IDs, see Home Assistant. The position is the one in the list, not on the device.
collapse false Whether the strip gives its fader to the strips after it while it has nothing to control, see Collapsing strips.

Device and application strips also have a label, see their pages.

Labels¶

A label is the top line of the LCD: at most 7 characters of ASCII, which is what fits. The bottom line shows the volume, or the state of the strip (muted, idle, off).

Where the label comes from:

Strip Label
output, input The label of the active profile, else label. Without both: the start of the device's description with device, "Output"/"Input" without it.
apps label (required).
auto The alias of the application from [aliases], or its name.
homeassistant What Home Assistant sets, until then label.

Volume range¶

The fader covers min_volume at the bottom to max_volume at the top. The encoder stays in that range too. For example, speakers that need amplification:

[[strip]]
type = "output"
device = "alsa_output.usb-Generic_USB_Audio-00.analog-stereo"
label = "Desk"
min_volume = 50
max_volume = 200
  • Above 100 % the sound server amplifies the audio in software.
  • min_volume must be below max_volume. Without max_volume the top is behavior.max_volume.
  • Profiles of a device strip can have a range of their own.
  • homeassistant strips have no range: Home Assistant decides what their fader means.

100 % at the 0 dB mark¶

The scale next to the faders of the X-Touch Extender marks 0 dB at about 77 % of the travel. The faders of strips that go above 100 % put 100 % there: from the bottom to the mark the fader covers min_volume to 100 %, from the mark to the top 100 % to max_volume. Both parts are linear. behavior.unity_at (default 77) moves the mark.

[behavior]
max_volume = 200   # the top 23 % of the travel amplify by up to 100 %
Fader A linear fader, 0..200 The faders here, 0..200
bottom 0 % 0 %
half way 100 % 65 %
0 dB mark (77 %) 154 % 100 %
top 200 % 200 %

With the default max_volume = 130 the two parts have the same slope: the fader is linear from 0 % to 130 %, with 100 % at the mark. Strips whose range ends at 100 % (max_volume = 100) are not affected: their 100 % is at the top. The GUI shows the option in "Global settings", "Behavior".

Elements of a strip¶

Element What it does on an audio strip
Fader Sets the volume. Follows changes made elsewhere. Only a touched fader changes the volume.
Encoder What encoder says, see Encoders.
Mute Toggles mute. Its LED is on while muted.
Rec, Solo Their LEDs are indicators. The buttons do nothing, except Rec with profiles.
Select On device strips: makes the device the default. On application and voip strips with launch: starts the application. Otherwise nothing on application strips.
LCD Top: the label. Bottom: the volume, or muted, idle, off. The backlight in color, dark while the strip has nothing to control.
Ring What the encoder controls.
Meter The level of what the strip controls, see Meters.

A strip with nothing to control (a device that is gone, an application that ended) goes dark, and its fader glides slowly to the bottom. When it is back, the fader goes up to its volume again.

Meters¶

The LED meter shows the peak level of what the strip controls, 10 times a second: from -60 dB (the lowest LED) up to just below full scale (the seventh LED), 5 dB per level of the device (about two levels per LED, see Hardware). A peak at full scale lights the red LED at the top too, for half a second. The device lets a meter fall by itself when no new level comes.

meter_point says where the level is taken, like the pre-fader and post-fader meters of a hardware mixer:

meter_point What the meter shows
"pre_fader" (default) The level before the strip's volume and Mute: the meter keeps showing the audio while the fader is down, and the Mute LED says whether it goes on.
"post_fader" The level after the volume and Mute: what you hear of an application, what goes out of the output, what applications get from the input. Dark while muted.

What each strip type can show:

Strip "pre_fader" "post_fader"
output Before its volume and Mute, as PipeWire's monitor of the output has it. Its monitor times its volume, nothing while muted.
apps, auto The streams' audio divided by their volume, so the fader doesn't move the meter. While muted the meter stays dark: PipeWire applies the volume and Mute inside the stream, before anything else can listen, so the audio of a muted stream is gone. The streams as they play.
input The input divided by its volume. Dark while muted, as above. The input as applications get it.
voip Not possible: the voice chat tells the level after the participant's volume (meter_point is refused on these strips and ignored from the layout and [behavior]).
homeassistant The meter entity, see Home Assistant.

The first strip that sets it wins: the strip's meter_point, then the one of its layout, then behavior.meter_point.

[behavior]
meter_point = "pre_fader"     # every strip ...

[[strip]]
type = "output"
meter_point = "post_fader"    # ... but this one: what goes out of the speakers

A level divided by the volume is exact while the volume stays. While the fader moves, the audio lags behind its volume by a few hundredths of a second: for 0.3 s after a change the meter divides by the loudest of the volumes since, so a fader pulled down does not light the red LED; the meter shows too low for a moment instead.

Empty strips¶

[[strip]]
type = "empty"

A dark strip that does nothing. Its fader stays at the bottom. It has no options besides type; it never collapses, since it is there to keep its place.
