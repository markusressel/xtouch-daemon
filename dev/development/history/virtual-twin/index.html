Design: the virtual twin¶

Status: built (2026-11-19), replaced by Previews, in the GUI or on the device (2026-11-19): one control shows edits and Simulate in the GUI (on the twin) or on the device. It added a twin to Try changes and situations, which stayed as it was, and replaced the drawing that the GUI made without a device (what it shows instead: A simulated device).

Problem¶

The GUI draws the device. It has three sources for that drawing:

Source Where the strips come from
The device, live The daemon's mirror of the device: every byte its Surface sent (Surface.mirror, live.server.strip_states).
Simulate, no "Try on the device" gui/domain/simulate.py: the GUI runs the resolver and paints a label and a color.
No daemon Was the same as Simulate: the file in its default situation (removed on 2026-11-19, the strips are dark now).

The second row is a second implementation of "what a strip looks like". It knows nothing of meters, rings, LEDs, faders, banners or the strip controllers, so it can only be wrong in new ways, and nothing can be played on it. The rule for the GUI is: the drawing is only ever the mirror of a device that the daemon drives. A device the user doesn't have is a virtual one, and the same holds for a situation the user doesn't have: it needs a device of its own that something drives.

Idea¶

A virtual twin: a second, complete set of strips that the daemon runs for the situation of Simulate, next to the real ones. The situation keeps its second use: with "Try on the device" on, the real device shows it too, as before. The twin is what Simulate draws and plays on in every case. It is built from the same parts as the real device (Surface, Desk, SlotMixer, SlotEngine, the strip controllers) and touches nothing real. The GUI asks for it and draws its mirror, exactly like the mirror of the real device. Without a daemon there is no twin, so no drawing: the GUI guides to the daemon.

flowchart LR
    subgraph Daemon
        direction TB
        RS[Surface] --> RM[mirror]
        RE[SlotEngine] --> RX[SlotMixer / Desk] --> RS
        SS[sound server] --> RE
        TW[World of a Situation] --> TN[SlotEngine]
        TN --> TX[SlotMixer / Desk] --> TS[Surface, no port] --> TM[mirror]
    end
    RM -- "Snapshot: strips, slots" --> GUI
    TM -- "TwinView: strips, slots" --> GUI

The twin has no port: a Surface writes everything it sends into its mirror whether a device is connected or not (Surface.send()), which is how the real mirror shows "what the device will show once it is there". A spike on 2026-11-19 confirmed it: a Surface on a port that never opens still mirrors texts, LEDs and rings. The twin needs no SimulatedDevice socket or transport; the mirror is its device.

What "touches nothing real" means¶

The twin is built from a Config and from nothing of the daemon: it gets no reference to the real backend, surface, bridge, voice chat provider, player list or state directory. That is the guarantee, not a check at each effect. What each part of the real path becomes:

Real Twin
AudioBackend (the sound server) TwinBackend: holds an AudioState, set_* change that state and report it like the sound server does.
AudioState Made from the situation and the real names (see The world). The real state is never passed on.
VoipProvider (the plugin) DemoProvider (the provider of the demo mode): people from the situation, volume and mute stay in its memory.
Meters (pw-record per target) A signal generator fed through the same Meter.feed_from(), scaled by the gain of the target.
Launcher (launch) The Launcher with a spawn that does nothing (it takes one already).
MQTT bridge, Home Assistant None: bridge=None. Home Assistant never sees the twin.
Media players (encoder = "media") None, as without a player.
state_dir (choices, people) None: no file is read or written.
Processes (states processes = [...]) None run; states are forced by the situation (overrides, as now).
Log The twin's engine logs at debug level under xtouch_daemon.twin.
Hands (buttons, faders, encoders) Play events for the twin go to its own Surface.play().

The world of a situation¶

A Situation (slots.outcomes: mode, on, flipped, people, apps, devices) is turned into an Observation for the real device by Situation.observation() (which stays, for "Try on the device"). The twin makes a whole world from it instead, and its engine goes through the normal path: Observer.observe(audio, processes, voip, now) is what a real step does.

world(scene, situation, names) -> World(audio: AudioState, voip: VoipState, processes: frozenset[str]), with names the applications that play and the people in the voice chat right now, as plain strings (see Decision 2):

Part of the scene In the world, unless the situation flipped it
output / input channel One device per entry of its devices: the first (or the second, ...) as a made-up node with a description of its own (Device 1), a volume of 100 %. A channel with a wildcard gets one device that matches it. Without devices the default one.
apps channel One playing stream whose name matches its first match entry (a preset: its first name).
person channel The person in the voice chat.
homeassistant channel Nothing: it is always present.
apps pool situation.apps playing streams: the real applications first, then App 1, App 2, ... (the pool flipped: all slots).
people pool situation.people people: the real ones first, then Alice, Bob, ...
devices pool situation.devices devices: the real ones that no channel has first, then Device 1, ...
Pools of channels Their channels are present as channels are; flipping the pool removes them all.
Pools of applications and of people of your own flipped: one stand-in that the pool takes (its match, its people); absent otherwise.

Everything else (states, modes) is the situation's on and mode: overrides and a mode picked in the twin's SlotEngine, as the real preview does it now. Delays and dwells apply as in reality (delay_on, absent_after, hold): that is the point of a twin that runs.

Decision 2: the twin takes the real names, and nothing else¶

The device that "Try on the device" drives shows real applications and people as stand-ins where it can (try-and-simulate decision 3), because it has audio behind them. The twin has no real audio behind anything, but it takes the same names first, so the twin and the device show the same words for the same situation. The daemon hands StartTwin's world the names as plain strings, copied; the twin holds no reference to the sound server, the voice chat or their state. Everything else in the world is made up.

The price: the strips of the twin depend on what plays right now, so the same configuration and situation give other strips at another time. Tests give the names.

The twin in the daemon¶

daemon/twin.py. Each client's twin is made by the daemon with the current names, and gets new ones when they change (Twin.set_names()), so a stand-in follows what plays:

class Twin:
    """The strips of a configuration in a situation, on a device of their own."""

    def __init__(self, config: Config, situation: Situation): ...
    def set_situation(self, situation: Situation) -> None: ...  # a new world, same engine and strips
    def set_names(self, names: Names) -> None: ...  # the real applications and people changed
    def reconfigure(self, config: Config) -> None: ...  # like a reload: ids carry over
    def play(self, event: StripEvent) -> None: ...  # a hand on the twin
    def view(self) -> TwinView: ...  # strips of the mirror + slots, states, why
    async def run(self) -> None: ...  # the steps, the meters; cancelled by close()
    def close(self) -> None: ...

It owns a Surface(port=NoPort()), a Desk(surface, chord, on_chord, capturer=pulse), a SlotEngine(config, state_dir=None, logger=...), a SlotMixer(surface, TwinBackend, config, engine, desk, DemoProvider, bridge=None, players=None, launcher=Launcher(spawn=...)), and a task that steps the engine like the daemon does (wake on a change of the world, the deadline of a delay or a dwell, every PROCESS_INTERVAL). As built, these parts, the loop and the step are one class that both are (Rig, daemon/rig.py); the daemon adds the real processes and Home Assistant to the step (_processes(), _stepped()), the twin its world.

The twin's strips are lit by the first step: Surface writes every color and text into its mirror, connected or not.

Seams that the twin needs in the existing code¶

Where Change
hardware/surface.py NoPort (a Port that is never there), hand(data) (bytes as if the device sent them). Nothing else: Surface already mirrors without a transport.
audio/meter.py, daemon/desk.py Meter(..., capturer=...) and Desk(..., capturer=...): the function that feeds peaks of a target (Capturer, with the gain it is told). The default is capture() (a process); the twin's feeds a fixed, slowly changing pattern while the target plays.
daemon/slots.py SlotEngine(..., logger=...). The situation (what_if, simulate(), active) stays: it is the real device's.
slots/outcomes.py, slots/world.py Situation.observation() stays for the real device; slots/world.py is new (the twin's world). simulate() and outcomes() stay: check-config and the table of Simulate's inputs use them.
voip/ DemoProvider.set_people(names): the people of the world, volume and mute of those who stay are kept.
daemon/lifecycle.py Twins by client, fed with the real names. preview() and snapshot().simulated stay.
live/messages.py, codec.py, server.py See The protocol.

The protocol¶

The snapshot is one line for every client. A twin belongs to one client, so its view is its own line, sent to its owner only.

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class StartTwin:
    """Run `config` (the whole TOML text) in `situation` on a twin of your own; again: the twin follows."""

    config: str
    situation: Situation


class KeepTwin: ...  # the heartbeat, as KeepPreview


class EndTwin: ...  # the twin is gone


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Play:  # gets `twin: bool = False`: the hand is on the twin
    event: StripEvent
    twin: bool = False


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class TwinView:
    config: str  # fingerprint(config) of the twin, as Snapshot.config
    strips: tuple[StripState, ...]
    slots: SlotsState  # slots, states, mode, why and who waits: the twin's
  • Replies: Reply("twin", ok, message) to StartTwin (the errors of the configuration as in "preview"), Reply("twin_ended", True, why) when the daemon ended it (heartbeat missed, the daemon stops).
  • The view line is {"twin": {...}}, sent by LiveServer.tell_view(client, view) whenever it changes (at first every INTERVAL; pushed since 2026-11-19) (meters and motors keep it changing, like the snapshot).
  • Feature twin in FEATURES, next to situation, which stays. An older daemon lacks twin: Simulate says it is too old and to restart it. Nothing else of the protocol changes.
  • One twin per connection. A twin ends with EndTwin, with its connection, and after TWIN_TIMEOUT (10 s) without KeepTwin.
  • Security: as StartPreview, only the user's own clients (peer_uid). The configuration text may hold secrets ([mqtt]); the twin ignores [mqtt] and [voip] providers (it builds a DemoProvider, never a socket).

The twin's own hands¶

A button, fader or encoder on the twin (Play, the mouse on its drawing) goes through its own Desk and changes the twin's AudioState through TwinBackend, and a pick encoder what a slot of auto shows. The mode and the states are the GUI's alone: the chord, a toggle: or mode: control and requests of modes and overrides change nothing on the twin (SlotEngine.fixed), so the bar of Simulate and the twin never disagree. (Planned first: the twin changes them and the GUI follows; dropped, two owners of the what-if.)

The GUI¶

  • Simulate needs a daemon of this user that takes twin. Without one the page says why in its place (like "Try on the device" does, unavailable()), with the same guidance: start the daemon, or "Try without a device". There is no Simulate without a daemon.
  • It needs no "Try on the device", and no daemon that runs this file: the configuration it sends is the document's, so Simulate works on a new file, or on a daemon that reads another one.
  • While Simulate is open the GUI sends StartTwin(document.render(), situation) a moment after each edit or click (DEBOUNCE_MS) and KeepTwin every HEARTBEAT_MS; a document with problems sends nothing (the twin keeps the last one that parsed, the problems show as they do).
  • The drawing shows the TwinView strips while Simulate is open, with the accent frame ("simulated"), and the real mirror otherwise. Between the request and the first view the drawing keeps what it had.
  • The cards (what a slot shows, why, who waits) are the twin's SlotsState. gui/domain/simulate.py shrinks to WhatIf (a value the user edits), Inputs (what a what-if can change) and presence_text; simulated() and simulated_faces() are deleted.
  • Play on the drawing sends Play(event, twin=True) while Simulate is open: the faders and buttons of the twin, never the real device.
  • "Try on the device" (the switch) is as it is: the unsaved configuration, and while Simulate is open its situation, on the real device. The twin runs either way.
  • Esc ends Simulate: EndTwin.

Decisions (2026-11-19)¶

  1. Simulate still reaches the real device with "Try on the device" (try-and-simulate decision 1 stands). The twin is added; it replaces only the drawing that the GUI made itself.
  2. The twin takes the real names, and nothing else (see Decision 2).
  3. One twin per client, no limit in the daemon.
  4. Simulate needs a daemon: no daemon, no twin, no drawing, only the guidance.

Implementation order¶

  1. Seams: NoPort, hand(), Meter/Desk capture, SlotEngine logger, Rig (daemon/rig.py).
  2. slots/world.py and TwinBackend, DemoProvider; Twin, unit-tested without a daemon: a twin of the example configuration shows its slots; the real backend of the daemon is never called.
  3. Protocol: messages, codec, LiveServer.tell_view, the daemon's twins.
  4. GUI: Simulate on the twin, the drawing, the cards, Play; delete simulated() / simulated_faces().
  5. Docs: the GUI guide (Simulate), try-and-simulate (marked as replaced in part), architecture (live view), changelog.

Tests¶

  • World: each row of the table, for the example configuration and for pools of your own.
  • Inertness: a daemon with spies for the backend, the voice chat provider, the bridge and the launcher. A twin with a situation, Play on its strips (volume, mute, launch), a reconfigure: none of the spies is called, and the real strips and the real SlotsState are unchanged.
  • Names: the same situation and names give the same TwinView strips (the meter pattern is a function of time since the start; tests set it); new names reach the stand-ins.
  • Protocol: codec round trips; a second client does not get the first one's view; the heartbeat; the end with the connection.
  • GUI: Simulate with a fake live daemon: sends StartTwin, draws its view, shows the cards of the view, Play sends twin=True; without a daemon the page guides and the drawing is dark.
