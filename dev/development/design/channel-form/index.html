Design: the form of a channel¶

Status: built (2026-11-19), not released; the meter is open (see Open). Builds on Previews of the controls and Output choices.

Problem¶

The form of a channel works in a wide window and falls apart in a narrower one (about 900 px for the inspector):

  • Choices are a line of fields each: label, device, card, profile, min and max volume, three ▾ buttons and ×. The line is wider than the card, so the whole card scrolls sideways and the help under every option is cut off. The label field shows 5 of the 7 characters the LCD has; the device and card fields show the end of a name.
  • Names (device, card, profile) are a text field with a completer that opens only after typing, and a ▾ menu beside it. Neither says which of the known devices the text matches, and wildcards (bluez_output.*) are a guess.
  • Known devices are only the ones the sound server has now. A device of another profile of the card (the digital output while the card is in its analog profile) and a Bluetooth device that is not connected are missing, although they are what choices are for.
  • Numbers in tables (min_volume, max_volume) are text fields.
  • The labels of choices and profiles go on the LCD, but nothing shows them there: the Display card shows the label of the channel only.
  • Remove is × on lines and a trash can on candidates of the slot cards and in the list.
  • Options that are off by default read as on/off: select and meter are switches, meter_point an empty combo box (unset, it shows nothing instead of the default it takes).
  • What the channel is shows only in its title ("Output"): what a type does (STRIP_TYPE_MEANINGS) shows in the Add menu only. Slots says what a slot is at the top of its form; States has the type as buttons in the middle.

Idea¶

1. The top of the form¶

Title, id and "Used by" as now, then one sentence of what the type is (STRIP_TYPE_MEANINGS), with "Learn more", like Slots. E.g. "An output device (speakers, headphones): the fader sets its volume. One device, one of several, or whatever is the default output." The cards below then read in that context: the meter of an output shows what plays on it. States gets the same: the type and its sentence at the top, the type buttons move there.

2. A form that fits¶

  • An option row has its name left of the field (144 px) while the card is wide enough for the name, the field and its help (at least 320 px for the field, option_row.FIELD_WIDTH); below that, the name goes above the field. Help always wraps, nothing in a card is wider than the card.
  • A list of tables (choices, profiles) is a list of small cards, one per entry, not a line of fields:
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⠿  Phones                                           ✎  🗑 │
│    Analog Output (Family 17h HD Audio)                   │
│    needs the card in "Analog Stereo Output"              │
│    20 – 130 %                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The label (the default label when there is none), the device by its description, what the choice needs and its volume range, each line only when it says something. ⠿ drags to reorder (Ctrl+Up/Down too): the order is the order Select goes through. ✎ or a double click opens the entry in place as a small form, the same option rows as the rest of the form: Label (as wide as its 7 characters), Device, "Switch the sound card first" (card and profile, folded while unset), Volume (from, to). One entry is open at a time; Esc or a click outside closes it. - Numbers are spin boxes with their unit ("130 %"), empty while unset with the value they take as placeholder ("by default 130 %"); ⟲ unsets them, as on the other options. - The Display card steps through the labels the LCD shows: the channel's, then one step per choice or profile ("Select picked Phones", "Rec picked Digital"), following every key in the form. - Unset options show what they take, from where: "Before the fader (the default of [behavior])". For meter_point that is the empty combo box now. - Remove is the trash can wherever an entry goes (lines, entries, sources, aliases, candidates). × stays on chips, where it takes a word out of a field.

3. A field for names¶

One field for every option that names something the sound server or the voice chat has (device, card, profile, match, ignore, person, the aliases), replacing the text field, its completer and its ▾ menu:

  • A click or focus opens the list of what is known; typing filters it (by any part of the name or the description, any case) and keeps it open. Up/Down and Enter pick; Esc closes it and keeps the text.
  • Each entry has two lines: the description, and the name in monospace, with the parts that match the text highlighted in both, and a tag of where it is known from: "default", "now", "profile Digital Stereo", "seen 3 days ago". Presets come first, under their heading, as in the menus now.
  • Under the field, while the text is set: what it matches, with the matches highlighted. "Matches Speaker (Digital Stereo), not there now" or, for bluez_output.*, "Matches 2: WH-1000XM4, Buds Pro (the first one that is there)". "Matches nothing known" in the color of a warning (not an error: a device can come later).
  • Picking a device in a choice that is only known with another profile of its card fills card and profile too.

4. Known devices¶

What the field lists, besides what the sound server has now:

  • From the profiles of the cards: each profile of a card that is not active has devices it would make. PipeWire names them from the card and the profile (alsa_card.pci-0000_11_00.4 with output:iec958-stereo gives alsa_output.pci-0000_11_00.4.iec958-stereo), for ALSA cards with the usual profiles; UCM profiles (HiFi) and pro audio name them differently, and get no entry. Tagged "profile ": nothing is stored, it is known as soon as the card is.
  • Remembered: the daemon keeps every device it saw in its state directory (name, description, direction, card, the active profile, when last seen), and sends the list to the GUI with the snapshot. Bluetooth headphones that are off, a USB headset that is unplugged, the digital output after it was used once. Tagged "seen ". A device not seen for 90 days is forgotten; the list has "Forget" on each remembered entry.

5. The buttons¶

Select, Rec and Solo of a channel each get a table: what a press does, what a hold does (longer than 0.5 s) and what the LED shows, with the actions of [controls] of the slots (one vocabulary for both):

[channel.main.select]
press = "default"   # make it the default; when it is, the next one (as now)
hold = "next"       # show and control the next device, the default stays
led = "default"

[channel.main.rec]
press = "profile"   # the next profile (as now with profiles)
led = "recording"
Action For Does
default output, input Makes the device shown the default; when it is, the next one of device/choices.
next output, input The strip shows (and controls) the next one, the default stays.
profile output, input The next of profiles.
launch apps, auto Starts launch (as now).
mute_others all audio While held, the other strips are muted (a solo).
talk input While held, Mute is off (push to talk).
homeassistant, cycle_modes, mode:<m>, toggle:<s>, { launch = [...] } all As in [controls].
off all Nothing.

The LED takes the indicators of rec and solo now (recording, playing, default, ...), plus shown for next. select = true/false and rec = "recording" (an indicator) keep working as shorthands: the first is press = "default"/"off", the second led. The Buttons card becomes a row per button: what a press does, a hold, the LED; its preview follows the actions.

6. The meter¶

One choice instead of the switch and the combo box: "Level before the fader" (default), "Level after the fader", "Off", "As in [behavior]". The type sentence at the top (1.) says what the level is of. More than that (a peak hold, a range) only if it turns out to be missing.

Order¶

  1. Built. GUI only: the top of the form, a form that fits, the list of cards with LCDs (and the Display card stepping through the labels of the choices), spin boxes, unset options, trash cans, the meter as one choice (the configuration keeps meter and meter_point).
  2. Built. The field for names, with what the sound server has now and the devices of the profiles (fields/names.py; the matching in domain/sources.py). The matches line is under device, card and profile of a choice; the lists of chips (device of a channel) have the list without it.
  3. Built. Remembered devices (runtime/device_memory.py, a line known_devices of the live view of its own rather than a field of every snapshot, and ForgetDevice).
  4. Built. The buttons (config/buttons.py; VolumeStrip.on_button/act() in strips/base.py, the kinds of strips add do(); mute_others mutes through the binder, the mode actions go to the SlotEngine through StripEnv.actions). Where it differs from 5:
  5. The LED takes press too (what the press does: the default, the profile, the application runs, the mode, held), the default of Select, and of Rec with profiles.
  6. A place in [slots] sets only the LED of a button: what a button does belongs to the channel.
  7. homeassistant sends events for the press and the release (select_pressed, ...); its LED is led, not an entity of Home Assistant. talk, mute_others and homeassistant last while held and take no hold.
  8. On the strips of the chord, Solo does the chord as well as its action.
  9. The GUI: a row per button with three lists (press and hold take typed actions too), the defaults of the type as placeholders; a part set alone keeps the short form (rec = "muted", select = false).

Decisions (2026-11-19)¶

  1. Remembered devices are kept by the daemon: it sees every device, also while no window is open.
  2. A table per button (press, hold, led); the flat options of now stay as shorthands.
  3. All actions of the table above in the first version: default, next, profile, those of [controls], talk, mute_others.
  4. The labels of choices and profiles show in the Display card, a step each; the list of entries has no LCDs.

Open¶

  • The meter: is "what it shows" (before/after the fader, off) enough, or should it take more (peak hold, range)?
