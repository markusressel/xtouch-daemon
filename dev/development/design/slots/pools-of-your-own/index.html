Pools of your own¶

Part of Design: channels, states and slots. Status: built (2026-11-19). Without kind, a [pool.<id>] lists channels (Pools); the pools of applications, people and devices are built in. This page adds pools of applications and of people that the user defines.

Why¶

The built-in apps pool hands out every application that plays and has no slot, in the order they started. That is one pool for everything. Wishes it can't express:

  • "The music players on slot 5, whatever else plays": apps would put a browser tab there as well.
  • "My friends first, on the left": the pool people has one order, one side and one look for everybody.
  • Two groups of applications on different sides of the device, each in its own color.

A channel can't do it either: an apps channel is one strip over all its streams, and one fader moves them together. What is missing is a pool that selects the applications (or people) that qualify, and still gives each its own strip.

The format¶

[pool.<id>] gets an optional kind. Without it, the section is a pool of channels, as today, so no file changes.

[pool.music]
kind = "apps"
match = ["@music"]            # presets and parts of names, as in an `apps` channel
exclude = ["spotify-ads"]     # optional: never these, though they match
from = "left"                 # the side it fills its slots from (default)
hold = 5                      # seconds a slot keeps an application after it stopped (default)
color = "green"               # the look of its strips, as in [pool.apps]

[pool.friends]
kind = "people"
people = ["Alice", "Bob"]     # names in the voice chat; this order is their order
from = "right"                # default: left for apps, right for people, as the built-in pools
color = "yellow"
talking_color = "green"

[slots]
5 = ["music", "apps"]         # a music player if one plays, else the next other application
6 = ["friends", "people"]     # Alice and Bob first, then anyone else in the call

Two kinds, no mixed pool. A pool of applications and a pool of people share a slot by being two candidates of its stack (["friends", "music", "auto"]), as the built-in ones do.

Option kind = "apps" kind = "people"
match Required. Parts of application names or binaries, presets allowed. Not allowed.
exclude Optional, as in [pool.apps]. Not allowed.
people Not allowed. Required. Names as in person of a channel (case-insensitive, whole names).
from Default left. Default right.
hold Seconds, default 5. Not allowed: people stay absent_after seconds, like the built-in pool.
look (color, meter, meter_point, rec, solo, lit_when_idle) The look options of [pool.apps]. The look options of [pool.people] (talking_color too).
encoder, min_volume, max_volume As in [pool.apps], but no pick; default media. Not allowed: the strips of people have none.

Errors use the existing words: a missing match is pool.music: kind apps needs match; an option of the other kind names where it belongs (people: an option of kind people). A kind other than the two lists them.

What it takes¶

Every member belongs to one source, and the first source that qualifies takes it. The order is fixed, from the most specific to the most general:

  1. Channels. An application of an apps channel that has a slot, and a person of a person channel, are the channel's, as today.
  2. Pools of your own, in the order of the file: pools of people, then pools of applications. A member that two of them could take goes to the one that comes first in the file.
  3. The built-in pools. apps and auto take the applications that nothing above took, people and auto the people.

So music takes a music player out of apps and auto, as an apps channel with a slot already does. A pool of your own takes its members only while a slot names it (in any stack, shown or not), like a channel with a slot. A pool that no slot names takes nothing, so a forgotten [pool.music] can't make the players disappear from the device; check-config says "pool music is on no slot".

auto stays what it is: the people and the applications that no channel and no pool of your own took.

How a slot is filled¶

The resolver already hands members out in rounds, by kind (slots/resolver.py, _contest): per kind the slots that take it, each kind from its side, and a slot that two kinds could fill goes to the one higher in its stack. This page keeps those rules and changes what a "kind" is: today it is one of people, apps, devices, in future it is a source, and every pool of your own is one.

  • Chain and hand-down are unchanged: a pool with no member for a slot hands it to the candidate below it (a pool or -). A pool of your own is a pool in a chain like the others, and may be above or below any of them. The rule that channels end a chain stays.
  • Contest. A slot named by several sources goes to the source higher in its stack; a tie goes to the order of What it takes (your pools before the built-in ones). The loser gives the slot up and hands out again, until nothing changes. It ends for the same reason as today: a source only loses slots.
  • Fill order. Each source from its side. The people of a pool of people first take the slots where it is the first candidate (Not a block); a pool of applications keeps the slot each had.
  • Pins and pick. A pool of applications has no pick encoder (encoder = "pick" is an error): its default is media. Pins of the built-in pools work unchanged.
  • Waiting. Members that got no slot wait per source: Result.waiting keys are pool names, so why and the GUI say "Alice waits" for friends and "Spotify waits" for music.
  • Presence. present of a pool of your own is true while it has a member (an application plays, a listed person is in the voice chat), counted before slots are assigned, like the built-in pools.

Hold, absence and reload¶

hold and absent_after become per source: PoolMembers (slots/inputs.py) keeps one Lingering list per pool of your own, not one for apps. A reload carries them over by pool id (carry); a pool that is gone drops its list.

In the daemon¶

Where Change
slots/model.py PoolConfig becomes a union on kind: channels (no kind), apps, people. Scene knows the pools of your own, their order, their members' rules (match, people) and their look. The names apps, people, auto, devices stay reserved.
slots/inputs.py Membership by the order above; PoolMembers per pool; pool_present for the new kinds.
slots/resolver.py KINDS, ORDER, kind_of: from three constants to the sources of the scene. Shown.pool is the pool of your own; Result.waiting keys likewise.
slots/outcomes.py, why.py, simulation.py The table of check-config and Simulate count presence of the new pools; the notes say "pool X is on no slot" and "pool X can never show" (like a channel below a pool). Simulate gets counts for the members of each pool of your own, as for apps and people.
runtime/slots.py, strips/ A strip for a member of a pool of your own is built like one of apps or people, with the look of its pool.
live/messages.py, mqtt.py SlotView.pool carries the pool id; the HA sensor sensor.<ns>_slot_<n> shows app:<name> / person:<name> as before, with the pool in its attributes.
GUI The Channels page gets "Pool of applications" and "Pool of people" in its Add menu and a form per kind (match, exclude or people, the side, the look). The slot card's chip names the pool and the member (music · Spotify).
Docs configuration/slots.md (pools), channels/applications.md and people.md, concepts.md, the example in config.example.toml stays small.

Use cases¶

Applications are listed in the order they started; A, B are people in the order of people.

U1: music apart from the rest¶

[pool.music] kind = "apps", match = ["@music"], 5 = ["music", "apps"], 6 = "apps".

Plays Slot 5 Slot 6
Firefox Firefox dark
Spotify Spotify dark
Firefox, Spotify Spotify Firefox
Spotify stops, hold passes (Firefox plays on) dark Firefox

With only Firefox playing, music has no member and hands slot 5 down to apps, which fills from the left. When Spotify comes, music takes slot 5 back and Firefox moves to slot 6, the one slot left for apps. Once Spotify is gone, Firefox keeps slot 6 (applications keep their slot) and slot 5 goes dark: apps has no other member.

U2: friends first¶

[pool.friends] kind = "people", people = ["Alice", "Bob"], 6 = ["friends", "people"], 7 = ["people", "apps"]. In a call with Bob, Carol and Alice: slot 6 Alice (the first of the pool's own order), slot 7 Carol, Bob waits for friends' second slot, which does not exist. Bob is not handed to people either: a member of a pool of your own stays in it, so why says "Bob waits".

U3: a pool above auto¶

3 = ["music", "auto"]: the players first, else the next application or person, as ["apps", "people"] would. The pool of your own wins a contest over auto on the same slot because it is higher in the stack.

Decisions to confirm¶

  1. Members are kept in their pool (U2): a person of friends who finds no slot waits, and does not fall into people. This keeps "who sits where" predictable and mirrors a channel with a slot. The alternative (fall through to the built-in pool when the slot's chain has one) gives Bob a slot, but then friends is no longer "the friends' strips".
  2. A pool of your own takes its members only while a slot names it. The alternative, always taking them, hides applications silently.
  3. kind = "devices" is not offered. The built-in devices pool has exclude already; a pool of devices of your own has no use case yet.
  4. No order in a pool of people. The list of names is the order. Members of the built-in people pool that are not listed sit after, as today.
