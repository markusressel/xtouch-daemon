Architecture¶

How the daemon works inside, for developers. To set up the project and run the tests, see Contributing. The other pages of this section: Audio, Live view, GUI, Testing.

graph LR
    Device[X-Touch Extender<br/>/dev/snd/midiC*D0] <-- "raw MIDI (midi.py, protocol.py)" --> Surface
    Surface -- "events: fader, touch, buttons, encoders" --> Desk
    Desk -- "to the controller bound to the strip" --> Mixer[SlotMixer]
    Mixer -- "set_fader, set_text, set_led ..." --> Surface
    Backend[PulseBackend<br/>one pulsectl-asyncio connection] -- "AudioState snapshot after every change" --> Mixer
    Mixer -- "set volume, mute, default" --> Backend
    Engine[SlotEngine<br/>slots/] -- "what each slot shows" --> Mixer
    Backend <--> Sound[pipewire-pulse]
    Desk -- "captures (meter.py), set_meter" --> PwRecord[pw-record, passive]
    Mixer -- "entities of channels, states, slots" --> Bridge[MqttBridge<br/>aiomqtt]
    Bridge <-- "discovery, states, commands, events" --> Broker[MQTT broker / Home Assistant]
    Daemon -- "owns, reloads, stops" --> Surface & Backend & Mixer & Bridge
    Surface -- "what the strips show (live/)" --> Live[LiveServer<br/>$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/xtouch-daemon.sock]
    Live -- "a JSON line per change" --> GUI[xtouch-daemon gui]
    GUI -- "mode, override, preview, play" --> Live
    Daemon -- "steps (runtime/slots.py)" --> Engine
    Voip[VoipProvider<br/>voip/] -- "participants" --> Engine & Mixer

Modules¶

The packages of src/xtouch_daemon/, from the device up. Tests mirror them: tests/<package>/test_<module>.py.

Module Role
hardware/protocol.py The Mackie Control codec, both directions. Pure functions.
hardware/midi.py Finds the device's sound card in /proc/asound/cards; reads and writes its raw MIDI device without blocking.
hardware/transport.py How the Surface reaches the device: AlsaPort (the raw MIDI device), SocketPort (the virtual device's socket).
hardware/strip.py Strip: what a strip shows, sending only what changed; the motor fader glides to its target and stays still while touched. VirtualStrip: a strip that waits off the device.
hardware/surface.py Surface: the connection to the device (again after it was unplugged), its events, touches, the startup and shutdown sequences. Keeps a SimulatedDevice as the mirror of the device, which the live view shows.
hardware/selftest.py The test of the device: the sweep, then the TestBench (every strip answers its controls), for xtouch-daemon test and the daemon (StartTest).
audio/ model (AudioState, an immutable snapshot of devices, streams and defaults), backend (AudioBackend, the interface of the strips), pulse (PulseBackend), meter (levels from passive pw-record captures, see Audio), media (MPRIS players, for encoder = "media").
config/ The TOML configuration, validated with pydantic: files, model (Config), sections, slots (the strip of every candidate), the strip types (strip, strips, audio_strips, voip_strips), values, names and presets (Design: presets).
strips/ The controllers of a strip: DeviceStrip (ChoiceStrip for choices), AppGroupStrip, AutoStrip, VoipStrip (a person), HomeAssistantStrip, EmptyStrip, on the base VolumeStrip. A controller gets its Identity (entity ids and names) and its Look (label, color, meter, LEDs) from the binder (look_of()).
slots/ Channels, states and slots without the device: model, expressions, inputs (presence), timing, states, resolver, simulation, outcomes, why, recording and replay. Pure but for the recorder.
runtime/rig.py Rig: the strips of a configuration on one surface (Desk, SlotEngine, SlotMixer), the loop that steps the slots, the requests of modes and overrides and the chord. The Daemon is the rig of the device, a virtual twin (runtime/twin.py) one on a device that is never there.
runtime/daemon.py Daemon: the lifecycle (signals, reload, preview, shutdown, systemd notifications) and the requests of the live view.
runtime/desk.py Desk: the strips of the device for as long as the daemon runs. Hands each event to the controller (Presenter) bound to its strip and a release to whoever got the press; follows the hands and the chord; runs the meters of what is bound now. A claim ([controls]) takes the controls it names (mask()).
runtime/slots.py, runtime/slot_files.py SlotEngine: steps a Simulation (0.1 s after a change, at the deadlines of its delays, every 2 s for the processes), takes the requests (mode, override, pick and pin), keeps overrides, the mode picked (slots.json) and the people (people.json) across restarts (what the daemon remembers), and carries its state over a reload by id.
runtime/binder.py SlotMixer: a controller per channel for as long as the configuration, per application of auto and per person without a channel while they are members. After each step it binds only the slots whose result changed.
runtime/controls.py Controls: the [controls] of the slots and their entities; the first entry of a slot whose when holds claims its controls on the Desk.
runtime/homeassistant.py SlotEntities: the entities of the mode, the states and their overrides, the channels shown and the slots, published after each step when they changed.
homeassistant/ The API of Home Assistant: client (HomeAssistant, one WebSocket connection for the daemon: it follows all entities and calls services; fetch_entities() once), states (EntityState), parts (what a part shows of an entity), domains (the value and service of each domain). strips/entity.py makes lines and values of them, next to the programs of strips/value.py.
mqtt.py MqttBridge: announces the entities (haad-mqtt-autodiscovery), restores the retained states, passes commands on, publishes availability.
simulator/ The virtual device: device (SimulatedDevice, without IO) and server (on a Unix socket). See Design: a simulated device.
system/ processes (from /proc), launch (Design: launching applications), instance (the runtime file and sockets of the running daemon), systemd (notify, journal levels, the unit, the user service).
live/ The live view: messages, codec (lines of JSON), server (LiveServer), device_test (xtouch-daemon test through a running daemon), log (the records the GUI shows). See Live view.
voip/ VoipProvider: the people of a voice chat, with DemoProvider and BridgeProvider (a client plugin on a Unix socket). voip/mumble.py and integrations/mumble/ build and install the Mumble plugin (C). See Design: the voice chat.
cli/ The command line: a module per topic, put together by main in cli/__init__.py.
gui/domain/ The editor without a toolkit. See GUI.
gui/qt/ The widgets (the gui extra): window/, preview/ (the drawing of the device), pages/, fields/, theme/.

Data flow¶

  1. PulseBackend subscribes to the events of the sound server. After a burst of events it takes one snapshot (AudioState) and hands it to the mixer.
  2. The SlotEngine resolves the slots; the SlotMixer binds the slots whose result changed and calls update(state) on every controller, which shows it on its Strip.
  3. The Surface sends only what changed. A fader target starts a glide that moves the motor every 10 ms.
  4. An event from the device goes from the Surface to the Desk, which hands it to the controller bound to its strip. The controller turns it into commands to the backend, run as tasks: nothing blocks the event loop.
  5. The sound server reports the result as a new snapshot: back to step 2.

A homeassistant channel ignores the snapshots. Its entities work as in haad: Home Assistant sends a command, the entity publishes it as its state (retained), and the copy that comes back from the broker changes the entity and the strip. The device's events go the other way: a fader or encoder publishes its number (a fader moved by hand at most every 100 ms), buttons publish events, which are not retained.

Lifecycle¶

  • Start: the daemon starts the surface, the backend and the mixer as tasks and tells systemd READY=1. The device and the sound server need not be there: both are connected when they appear.
  • Reload (SIGHUP, or a change of the file): the file is read again. One with errors is logged and the current configuration stays. _watch_file() polls the file's mtime and size (FILE_CHECK, 1 s) and reloads once a new stamp was seen twice; a stamp that a reload took already (e.g. the GUI's, which reloads after saving) and a file that is gone are no change. Otherwise a new SlotMixer takes over the same surface and backend; the SlotEngine carries its states, picks and people over by id, so slots that show the same keep it. systemd learns of it through RELOADING=1 and READY=1.
  • Preview: a configuration of the GUI takes over like a reload, from memory instead of the file (see Live view).
  • Stop (SIGTERM, SIGINT): STOPPING=1; first everything stops that could show something (the sound server, MQTT, the mixer, the device's events), then the strips go dark and the faders down (waiting at most 2 s), and only then the surface closes. A snapshot arriving during the reset would otherwise paint the strips again. A dark surface shows that nothing drives it.
  • MQTT: the bridge connects (again) by itself. On every connect it publishes the discovery configurations, subscribes, and waits for the retained states with a ping on its own sync topic (the broker sends retained messages before later ones); entities without one get their defaults. A reload removes the entities of removed channels from Home Assistant (a preview doesn't). A changed [mqtt] needs a restart.
  • Status: systemctl --user status xtouch-daemon shows whether the device, the sound server and the broker are connected.

Decisions¶

  • One snapshot for all strips. Every strip sees the same consistent state, and one connection serves eight strips. The strips derive everything from it instead of keeping copies.
  • Coalesced volume commands. A fader sends many positions a second. A strip sets only the latest one when the previous command finished, and shows it at once, so the display does not lag behind the hand.
  • The default device follows by name. A strip without device looks up the default in every snapshot, so it and its meter follow a switch of the default.
  • One owner per strip. A strip belongs to the sound server or to Home Assistant, by its type; two sources would fight over the motor.
  • Availability follows the device. The entities are available while the daemon runs and the device is connected, so Home Assistant greys out controls that would do nothing. A last will covers a crash.
  • haad-mqtt-autodiscovery as a path dependency, as in haad: the same entities, from a checkout next to this one (which also sets Python 3.14).
  • No volume restore. The sound server keeps the volumes. The daemon remembers only the volume of an idle application strip, for its next stream, while it runs.
  • Channels, states and slots (design): the SlotEngine alone decides what shows where (the pick encoder and pins are requests to it), the SlotMixer only shows it. Slots held on the Desk are fixed inputs of the resolver, so nothing changes under a finger. A slot that shows something else flashes its label and why for switching.banner seconds; a new mode shows its banner across the LCDs instead.
